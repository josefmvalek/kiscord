# Kiscord Modules Overview

> Central directory of all 55+ modules and channels in Kiscord organized by category.

---

## 🏛️ Category Structure

### 0. [📌 Daily Routine (Můj Den)](./core-features.md)
Core daily management and tracking:
- **`#dashboard`** (`dashboard.js`) — Personal hub, sunflower sync, hydration, sleep tracker, daily notes.
- **`#kalendář`** (`calendar.js`) — Monthly planner for dates, university schedule, and memories with mood heatmap.

### 1. [🎓 VUT FIT & Koleje Brno](./vut-fit.md)
Tools for university studies and dorm life in Brno:
- **`#rozvrh`** (`schedule.js`) — Weekly timetable with campus room hints and joint free window detector.
- **`#studijní-plán`** (`studyPlanner.js`) — WIS point tracking, exam countdowns, and project deadlines.
- **`#koleje-brno`** (`dormHub.js`) — Laundry room machine tracker, dorm packing checklist, and cafeteria menu radar.
- **`#finance`** (`financeTracker.js`) — Personal Brno budget and savings piggy bank goals.
- **`#počítač`** (`laptopComparison.js`) — Computer comparison matrix and student guide.

### 2. [🌿 Health & Fitness](./gym.md)
Fitness ecosystem and health tracking:
- **`#posilovna`** (`gym/`) — Complete gym tracker with active HUD, rest timer, 100+ exercises with GIFs, PRs, and muscle heatmap.
- **`#návyky`** (`habits.js`) — Daily habit streaks with Love Coins rewards.
- **`#regenerace`** (`regenerace/`) — Biohacking and supplement science guide.

### 3. [💖 Our World & Journey](./journey.md)
Couple life, shared memories, and milestones:
- **`#obchůdek`** (`loveShop.js`) — Couple coupon shop and redeemed coupon pantry.
- **`#plánovač-rande`** (`map.js`) — Interactive Leaflet map with pinned date spots and routing.
- **`#bucket-list`** (`bucketlist.js`) — Shared dream bucket list with photo uploads.
- **`#společné-questy`** (`quests.js`) — Long-term co-op missions and milestones.
- **`#denní-otázky`** (`dailyQuestions.js`) & **`#témata`** (`topics.js`) — Daily conversational prompts and deep-talk card decks.
- **`#timeline`** (`timeline/`) — Memory photo timeline with lightbox gallery, polaroid album mode, and date sorter.
- **`#dopisy`** (`letters.js`) — Time-locked message capsules for anniversaries.
- **`#achievementy`** (`achievements.js`) — Gamified relationship trophies.

### 4. [🎮 Entertainment & Arcade](./interactivity.md)
Multimedia hub and central two-player game lounge:
- **`#knihovna`** (`library/`) — Movies, series, and games catalogue with TMDB search, game modes (Stálice vs Backlog), and magnet links.
- **`#watchlist`** (`watchlist.js`) — Mutual matches, personal wishlists, **Tinder Matcher**, and **Dice of Chance**.
- **`#music-bot`** (`static.js`) — Shared music playlist and web player.
- **`#gamesky`** (`gamesHub.js`) — Central Arcade Hub with Draw Duel, Who Is More Likely To?, Couple Quizzes, Photo Puzzle, Tetris War Tracker, Tier Lists, and Fact Encyclopedia.

### 5. [📦 Archive](./education.md)
Historical chapters and work archives:
- **`#rakousko-kasička`** (`kasicka.js`) & **`#rakousko-info`** (`austriaInfo.js`) — Alpine work brigade earnings and guidelines.
- **`#plánovač-směn`** (`shifts.js`) & **`#rakouská-němčina`** (`austrianGerman.js`) — Shift scheduler and dialect flashcards.
- **`#matura-*`** (`matura.js`) — High school graduation study tracker and knowledge base.

### 6. [⚙️ System & Settings](./system.md)
Application preferences and analytics:
- **`#statistiky`** (`stats.js`) — Relationship analytics and activity metrics.
- **`#nastavení`** (`settings/`) — 7 visual themes, audio/haptic preferences, profile manager, sidebar editor, and cache tools.
- **`#changelog`** (`changelog.js`) — Version history and release notes.
