# Group D: Our World & Journey

> Modules dedicated to couple life, date planning, deep conversations, shared dreams, and memory preservation.

---

## 1. Love Shop & Coupon Pantry (`#obchůdek`)
- **File:** `js/domains/couple/love-shop/index.js`
- **Capabilities:**
  - Romantic coupon shop where users redeem **Love Coins** (earned from completing habits, workouts, and health goals) for custom vouchers (e.g. *Back Massage*, *Breakfast in Bed*, *Movie Night Choice*).
  - Coupon pantry for redeeming purchased vouchers with direct calendar event creation.

---

## 2. Date Planner & Map (`#plánovač-rande`)
- **File:** `js/domains/lifestyle/date-planner/index.js`
- **Capabilities:**
  - Interactive Leaflet map featuring pinned favorite spots, future wishlist destinations, and visited memories.
  - Route planning and external links to Google Maps navigation.

---

## 3. Real-Time Touch & Intimacy
- **`#dotek-na-dálku` (`js/domains/couple/haptic-touch.js`)**: Real-time Haptic Touchpad & heartbeat transmitter for long-distance connection 🫀.
- **`#párový-kvíz` (`js/domains/couple/couple-quiz.js`)**: Interactive quizzes testing couple memories and compatibility.
- **`#zpovědnice` (`js/domains/couple/confession.js`)**: Intimate prompts and confessions.

---

## 4. Shared Dreams & Co-op Quests
- **`#bucket-list` (`js/domains/lifestyle/bucketlist.js`)**: Shared bucket list with priority tags, category filters, and completion photo uploads.
- **`#společné-questy` (`js/domains/entertainment/quests.js`)**: Cooperative challenges with real-time aggregate progression.

---

## 5. Daily Conversations & Questions
- **`#denní-otázky` (`js/domains/couple/daily-questions.js`)**: Unlocks a daily question for both partners with historical answer archives.
- **`#témata` (`js/domains/couple/topics/`)**: Digital card deck with thought-provoking, deep, and fun conversation prompts.

---

## 6. Memories, Letters & Milestones
- **`#timeline` (`js/domains/lifestyle/timeline/index.js`)**: Chronological memory timeline with photo albums, captions, and lightbox gallery.
- **`#dopisy` (`js/domains/couple/letters/index.js`)**: Time-locked digital message capsules that remain sealed until a specific future date (e.g. anniversaries).
- **`#couple-wrapped` (`js/domains/couple/wrapped/`)**: Annual couple statistics and highlights.
- **`#achievementy` (`js/domains/entertainment/achievements.js`)**: Gamified relationship trophies with automated unlock triggers.
